# Emergency Triage Policy

City General Hospital uses a four-level severity scale for triage.

## Severity levels
- **Emergency**: Life-threatening. Seen immediately. Examples: cardiac arrest,
  stroke symptoms, severe difficulty breathing, anaphylaxis, major trauma.
- **High**: Urgent, seen within 30 minutes. Examples: chest pain without cardiac
  arrest signs, severe abdominal pain, high fever in infants.
- **Medium**: Seen within 2 hours. Examples: moderate pain, persistent vomiting,
  minor fractures.
- **Low**: Routine, schedule an appointment. Examples: mild rash, cold symptoms,
  medication refills, follow-ups.

## Virtual vs in-person
Low and medium severity dermatology, follow-up, and medication review
appointments are eligible for virtual consultation. Emergency and high severity
cases always require in-person assessment.
